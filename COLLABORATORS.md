# 🤝 Collaborators

How to work with the Wiki Platform
([README](./README.md)).

## Contact

- **Email:** [dev@lingadevaru.in](mailto:dev@lingadevaru.in)
- Use the subject `Wiki Platform: <topic>` and mention which of the
  [8 language sites](./README.md#eight-separate-websites-one-monorepo)
  it concerns.

## Ways to collaborate

- **Corrections** — a link to the page, a screenshot, and the expected vs.
  actual text is all it takes.
- **Translations** — native speakers reviewing any of the 8 locales are
  especially welcome, one reviewed dictionary at a time.
- **New languages** — propose a language with its native name and a sample
  paragraph; if accepted, it ships as a new subdomain like the rest.
- **Ideas & features** — describe the reader problem first, the solution
  second.

## Sponsors

The platform is independent work. If it helped you and you'd like to
support it, write to [dev@lingadevaru.in](mailto:dev@lingadevaru.in) with
the subject `Wiki Platform: sponsorship` — every supporter is credited
here by name (or anonymously, your choice).

## House rules

- Be kind and specific.
- No spam, no credentials in reports (see [Security](./SECURITY.md)).
- No scraping the sites — the dictionaries are human-reviewed work, and
  all eight sites are bot-protected.
- Response time: best effort, evenings IST. Shipped fixes appear in
  [`releases/`](./releases/).
