# Code of Conduct

How we treat each other around the Wiki Platform
([README](./README.md)).

## Promise

A welcoming space for readers, translators, and contributors of every
background and language. The platform itself spans 8 languages — its
community should be at least as inclusive.

## Standards

- Be kind, specific, and patient — many contributors write in a second
  language.
- Give credit: translators and reporters are acknowledged in release notes.
- No harassment, spam, hate speech, or scraping of the sites.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to
[dev@lingadevaru.in](mailto:dev@lingadevaru.in) with the subject
`Wiki Platform: conduct`. Reports are handled privately; repeated or
serious violations lead to being blocked from collaborating.
