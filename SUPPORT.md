# Support

How to get help with the Wiki Platform ([README](./README.md)).

- **Questions & ideas:** [dev@lingadevaru.in](mailto:dev@lingadevaru.in)
  with the subject `Wiki Platform: <topic>`.
- **Bug reports:** include the site URL, a screenshot, and expected vs.
  actual behavior. Never include credentials or private data.
- **Response time:** best effort, evenings IST.
- **Status & history:** [releases](./releases/) and
  [CHANGELOG](./releases/CHANGELOG.md) show what shipped and when.

For contribution rules see [Collaborators](./.github/COLLABORATORS.md);
for protection policy see [Security](./.github/SECURITY.md).
